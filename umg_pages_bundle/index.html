About UMG

Your Local Experts
in Kitchen & Bath Design

Universal Marble & Granite specializes in kitchen and bath design, offering cabinet sales, vanities, granite, quartz countertops, and more. Serving homeowners, contractors, and renovators, we combine quality materials with dependable service to bring your vision to life. Our exceptional craftsmanship and customer-first approach have made us a trusted name in Philadelphia, with a showroom and warehouse that showcase a wide selection of options.

Our goal is to make the process feel simple, elevated, and inspiring, whether you are updating a single room or planning a full renovation.

How It Works

Your Project,
Step by Step

From your first showroom visit to the finished install,
here’s exactly what to expect when you work with UMG.

01 Visit the Showroom ›

Stop by our Philadelphia showroom with your measurements to get started. No measurements? No problem — we can arrange to have your space professionally measured.

02 Pick Your Styles ›

Walk the showroom and compare cabinet lines, countertop slabs, and finishes side by side, with hands-on guidance from our team.

03 Get a Free 3D Design ›

We turn your measurements and selections into a free 3D kitchen design, so you can see your new space and confirm the layout before ordering.

04 Place Your Order ›

Confirm your design and place a half deposit to get your order moving. The remaining balance is due before pickup, delivery, or installation.
