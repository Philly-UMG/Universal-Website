Finish Categories

Browse the surfaces UMG works with.

A curated mix of flooring, tile, and decorative finish options — selected to pair cleanly with the cabinetry and countertops we install.

Hardwood

Hardwood Flooring

Solid and engineered hardwood in a range of species, widths, and stains — a warm, classic foundation that ages well.

Vinyl Plank

Luxury Vinyl Plank

Waterproof, scratch-resistant LVP and LVT — a durable, budget-friendly option that performs well in kitchens, baths, and basements.

Tile

Porcelain & Stone Tile

Porcelain, ceramic, and natural-stone tile for floors, walls, and showers — including large-format slabs and classic mosaic patterns.

Decorative

Backsplash & Finishes

Backsplashes, accent walls, and decorative finish details that pull the countertops, cabinets, and flooring together.

Vessel Sinks

Vessel sinks that finish a bath or powder room.

Hand-painted ceramic, artisan earth tones, and jewel-toned glass — statement bowls that pair with the tile, backsplash, and vanity tops UMG installs. All four collections are on display in our Philadelphia showroom.

Ceramic

Painted Ceramic

Folk-art motifs and detailed brushwork on classic round bowls — a decorative focal point for powder rooms and guest baths.

Artisan

Artisan Earth

Warm earth tones and calligraphic detailing in a matte artisan finish — a quieter option that sits well against natural stone and wood.

Glass

Jewel Glass

Tempered glass bowls in deep jewel tones, with clear and frosted finishes that catch light above a vanity top.

Mixed

Mixed Studio

A rotating mix of glass and ceramic styles across colors and finishes — useful when you want something unique to a single bath.
