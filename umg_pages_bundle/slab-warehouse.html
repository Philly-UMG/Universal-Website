About the Warehouse

A working warehouse, not a showroom.

Most of the surface you'll choose for your kitchen lives in our Philadelphia slab warehouse first. Full-size slabs lean against the racks, edges still rough from the quarry, waiting to be templated and cut. We bring clients in to walk the rows, lay hands on the stone, and pick the exact slab their countertop will come from.

It's a level of transparency you don't get from a sample swatch — you see the full movement, the natural veining, and the imperfections that make every piece one-of-a-kind. Then we fabricate it in-house and install it ourselves.

What's in Stock

Four stone categories. One warehouse floor.

From everyday-durable to once-in-a-lifetime statement pieces, we keep a working selection of each.

Granite

Natural, dense, and built to last. Heat-resistant, with deep movement and earthy color palettes that read traditional or modern depending on the finish.

Quartz

Engineered for consistency. Non-porous, low-maintenance, and available in clean modern colors and convincing marble-look patterns.

Marble

Soft, luminous, and full of character. The classic kitchen and bath surface — cool to the touch and unmistakably high-end.

Quartzite

Looks like marble, performs like granite. A harder natural stone with dramatic veining for clients who want the marble look without the upkeep.

How a Visit Works

From warehouse floor to finished install.

  1. 01

    Schedule a walk-through

    Give us a call or stop in. We'll let you know what's in stock so you know what to expect.

  2. 02

    Pick your slab

    Walk the warehouse with our team. Touch the stone, see the full movement, and choose the exact piece your countertop will come from.

  3. 03

    Template & cut

    We template your space and bring the layout back to fabrication. Cuts, edges, and seams are planned around the slab's natural veining.

  4. 04

    Install

    UMG installs the finished surface, sets the edges, and walks you through care. One team, start to finish.
