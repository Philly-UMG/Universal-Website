Surface Types

Explore surface types UMG offers.

A curated range of stone categories — granite, quartz, marble, and quartzite — offering flexibility across style, durability, and finish.

Granite

Granite Countertops

A durable natural-stone option with movement, depth, and a wide range of tones for kitchens, baths, and hardworking spaces.

Quartz

Quartz Countertops

A clean, versatile surface category with strong visual range, from bright marble-inspired looks to softer modern neutrals.

Marble

Marble Countertops

A more classic and elevated stone direction, often chosen for statement surfaces, vanities, and high-end interior applications.

Quartzite

Quartzite Countertops

A natural-stone surface prized for its hardness and marble-like veining — a refined option that stands up well to everyday wear.
