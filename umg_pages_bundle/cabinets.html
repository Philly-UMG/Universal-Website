Traditional Style Cabinetry

Detailed, classic, and warm.

UMG describes its traditional style cabinetry as elegant and timeless, with intricate detailing, symmetry, and a strong sense of craftsmanship. This section turns those offerings into a clean, browseable grid for a more elevated shopping feel.

Standard Features on All Styles

  • Full overlay, butt door
  • 1/2" A-grade plywood box with wood veneer
  • Concealed 6-way adjustable soft-close hinges
  • Dovetail drawer box, 21" length, natural finish
  • Undermount full-extension soft-close drawer glides
  • No center stile on 24"–36" wide cabinets

European Flat Panels

Minimal, streamlined, and modern.

UMG presents its European style cabinetry as clean-lined, frameless, and subtly luxurious. This section leans into that message with tighter cards, quieter surfaces, and a more modern visual rhythm.

Standard Features on All Styles

  • Frameless, full-overlay construction
  • 3/4" plywood / engineered wood box
  • 6-way adjustable soft-close hinges
  • Undermount full-extension soft-close drawer glides
  • Durable PET, lacquer, or textured melamine finish
  • Laser-edge banding for a seamless look
