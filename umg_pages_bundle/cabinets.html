Caring for Your Cabinets

A little love goes a long way.

Your cabinets are built to last, but a gentle touch keeps them looking new for years. Here are a few simple things to keep in mind — we’re always here if you have questions.

  • Wipe with a soft, damp cloth

    Skip harsh household cleaners and abrasive sponges — mild soap and water keeps the finish looking its best.

  • Keep them dry

    Wipe up spills quickly and avoid standing water or excess steam. Good ventilation around sinks and dishwashers helps a lot.

  • Expect a little character

    Natural wood expands, contracts, and ages beautifully. Slight variations in color and grain are part of what makes each piece one-of-a-kind.

  • Built for everyday life

    Designed for normal residential use. A little routine care keeps everything looking sharp through years of cooking, hosting, and busy mornings.

Questions about cleaning or upkeep? Give us a call or stop by the showroom — we’re happy to help.
